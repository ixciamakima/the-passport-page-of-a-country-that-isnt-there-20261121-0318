The Passport Page of a Country That Isn't There: an invented passport spread for the Republic of Valdoria, a landlocked mountain republic. The data page carries a vector portrait, bilingual Valdorian/English fields and an ICAO machine-readable strip; the visa page carries twelve arrival/departure stamps from the three neighbouring countries, in chronological order after the passport's issue date, plus a Kessar entry-condition stamp (90 days, no employment, fee paid 40 VLD) and the Valdorian high-altitude endorsement: travel above 2,500 m only with a registered mountain guide.
